How can we help?

Find quick answers about reminders, synchronization, photographs, and premium features.

Watering reminders are not appearing

  • Check that the plant has a next watering date and repeat interval.
  • Open iOS Settings and confirm that notifications are allowed for My Happy Plants.
  • In the app’s settings, confirm that daily watering reminders are enabled.

Plants are not synchronizing

  • Confirm that both devices use the same Apple Account.
  • Make sure iCloud Drive is enabled and the app is allowed to use iCloud.
  • Connect to the internet, reopen the app, and allow a moment for synchronization.

A photograph is missing

Check the app’s Photos permission in iOS Settings. If you expect a photograph to appear on another device, confirm that the relevant premium synchronization feature is active and iCloud is available.

Restore a premium purchase

Open the premium screen in the app and choose Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account that made the original purchase. Apple manages billing and purchase history.

Report a problem

When contacting support, please include your device model, iOS version, app version, and a short description of what happened. Do not send passwords, payment-card details, or other sensitive information.
