Privacy Policy

Your plants are your business.

My Happy Plants is designed to keep your plant information under your control. This policy explains what information the app uses and where it is stored.

Effective date: October 6, 2026

Information you provide

The app lets you enter plant and place names, assign plants to places, create watering schedules, and optionally add photographs. This information is used only to provide the app’s plant-management features.

Local storage

Your plant collection, settings, schedules, and photographs are stored on your device. The app does not require you to create a separate My Happy Plants account.

iCloud synchronization

If you use synchronization or backup features, your plant information and eligible photographs may be stored in Apple iCloud using your private CloudKit database. This allows your data to remain synchronized across devices signed in to the same Apple Account. Access to iCloud is governed by Apple’s terms and privacy policy.

Camera and photo library

With your permission, the app can use the camera or photo library so you can attach photographs to plants and places. The app accesses only the photographs you choose or capture. You can change these permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

Notifications

If you enable watering reminders, the app schedules notifications through iOS. Notification preferences and schedules are used only to remind you about plant care. You can disable notifications in the app or in iOS Settings.

In-App Purchases

Premium purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. The developer does not receive your payment-card or billing details. Apple may provide the app with purchase status and product information so that premium features can be unlocked or restored.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

My Happy Plants does not include third-party advertising, cross-app tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs. The app does not sell your personal information.

Data retention and deletion

Your local app data remains on your device until you remove it in the app or delete the app. Synchronized information may remain in your private iCloud storage according to Apple’s iCloud settings and retention practices. You can manage the app’s iCloud access through your Apple Account and iCloud settings.

Children’s privacy

The app is not designed to collect personal information from children. It does not provide social networking, public profiles, or user-to-user communication.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app’s features or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will identify the latest revision.
